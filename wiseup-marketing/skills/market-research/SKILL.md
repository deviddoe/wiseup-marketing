# Market research

Executed by `researcher`. For a large study the CMO may split it across parallel researcher runs, one per segment or per competitor group.

## Steps
1. **Question**: write the exact question and the decision it informs at the top of the file. A study with no decision behind it is not worth running.
2. **Search plan**: queries in every language from `00-config.md`. Always include the local-language terms a customer would actually type, plus competitors' own sites, pricing pages, app stores, social pages and map listings.
3. **Collect**: open every page you cite; record the URL and the access date. Snippets are not sources.
4. **Competitor profile table**: name, type, segment, price (converted to the project currency, with the rate and date shown), key features, does it speak the customer's language, how it markets itself, strengths, weaknesses versus us.
5. **Voice of customer**: short anonymised quotes of real pains from public groups and reviews, grouped by theme. Keep the original language and add a translation.
6. **So what for us**: 3-5 implications, each tied to the file it should change - a `_context/` document, a campaign, or a pricing question for the owner.
7. Save `research/YYYY-MM-DD_<topic>.md`, `status: draft`. If a fact in `_context/` turns out to be wrong, propose the edit to the CMO; never change `_context/` silently.
